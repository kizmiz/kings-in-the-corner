Kings in the Cornerand Devils in the Corner

A free card game for iPhone and Android. Up to four at the table: you and three of the family, or the family at home on the same Wi-Fi, each on their own phone. A green felt card table with a printed gold line, a classic deck with Bodoni indices and double-headed court cards. In Devils in the Corner the kings open one eye, the felt goes oxblood and the gold turns to ember: a red devil in a corner trades hands, a black one deals the sides into new piles.

The deck

Classic pip layouts, Bodoni indices in two corners, and court cards in a frame, mirrored top to bottom, in red, blue and gold. In Devils in the Corner each king opens a burning eye on his crown, with a small eye under the index, so a devil reads even half covered. Classic kings never have one.

On the felt

Refinements

From a round of design research: card games that feel good move cards like hands do, keep every target big enough for a thumb, never say anything by colour alone, and give a timer a warning, a way to ask for more, and an off switch. The rivals now take their time: a moment to think, then each card flies from their seat, lifts, glides and settles where it lands, and the line under the cross stays up until their next move.

Rival speed in Settings stretches the rivals' pace to 140% (Relaxed) or shrinks it to 55% (Quick); tapping the table during a rival's turn hurries the rest of it. With Reduce Motion on, cards cross-fade into place instead of travelling. No haptics for the rivals' moves, only for yours and for a devil's power.
